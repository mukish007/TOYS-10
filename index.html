Your privacy matters.

TOYS 10 is a front-end demo storefront. This page explains how this prototype handles information and what is not yet connected.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

01

Information you enter

The demo login accepts an email and password in your browser to show the interface. Do not use a real or reused password. This prototype does not send login details to a server.

02

Browser storage

The demo stores the entered email in session storage to simulate a signed-in state for the current browser tab session. The cart is kept in page memory and may reset when the page is refreshed.

03

Orders and payments

Checkout is only a demonstration. It does not submit a real order, process UPI or card payments, or send delivery details to a business. No real payment should be made through this demo.

04

Cookies and third parties

This standalone file does not intentionally use advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Hosting it later may introduce services with their own privacy practices.

05

Children and guardians

TOYS 10 is a toy-store concept. If this becomes a real store, a guardian-friendly privacy notice and appropriate protections for children should be implemented before collecting personal information.

06

Contact and policy updates

This prototype has no customer-support backend yet. Before launch, replace this section with your business contact details and a policy reviewed for the laws that apply to your store.
